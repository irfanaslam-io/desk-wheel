# Desk Wheel for larger companies

Desk Wheel is **free** for (see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md): your choice of the PolyForm Small Business or
PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0):

- **individuals**: at home, for study, hobbies and personal projects, and for your own freelance work;
- **small businesses**: fewer than **100 people** (employees and contractors together) **and** less than
  **US$1 million** revenue in the last tax year (the licence adjusts that figure for inflation);
- **charities, schools, universities, public research and health organisations, and government institutions**.

**Larger companies** (100 or more people, or US$1 million or more in revenue, counting their parent and sister
companies) need a **commercial licence** to use Desk Wheel for their work, or to build on its code.

To get one, write to **hello@irfanaslam.io** with your company name and roughly how many people would use it.
Not sure whether your company counts as small? Ask at the same address.
